Report a wrong figure

Every line of an answer carries its source (an official one wherever it exists) and the date it was checked. If a figure looks wrong, write to us with:

  • the country;
  • the line concerned (its name as shown, for example "Statutory pension insurance"), or the tool and the question you asked;
  • the source that shows the right figure (a link to the official text or page), if you have one.

We check the official source. If the figure is wrong, the dataset is corrected with a new check date, and the change appears on eorscope.com and in the tools.
