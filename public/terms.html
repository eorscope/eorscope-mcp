What the service is

A cost comparison. The tools return the statutory employer cost of employing someone in a covered country (employer social contributions, payroll taxes and mandatory levies), built from official rules and exchange rates on the dates shown in each answer. The service is free, needs no account and only reads data: it does not hire, pay, sign up or contact anyone.

What it is not

It is not legal, tax or employment advice. Figures are estimates on the dates shown; rules change, and a real payroll can depend on facts the tools do not know. Confirm every number with the provider and a qualified adviser before hiring.

The service gives no provider's price. Any Employer of Record fee, entity cost or contractor fee in an answer is the figure you supplied. Countries and dated changes that the dataset does not cover are reported as such (for example "not yet tracked"); that does not mean nothing applies.
